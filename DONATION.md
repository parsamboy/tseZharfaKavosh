# Donation

**Optional. Voluntary. Not tracked. Not a condition of use.**

---

## What This Is

This document describes the optional donation channel of the
`tseZharfaKavosh` project.

Donation allows supporters to fund continued development. It
is entirely voluntary. Users who do not donate retain all
rights granted by Smart-FFA-1.1.

---

## What Donation Is

- A way to say thank you.
- A way to support continued development.
- A contribution to infrastructure costs such as hosting,
  testing, and code-signing certificates.
- A voluntary transfer.

## What Donation Is Not

- Not a license fee.
- Not a subscription.
- Not a condition of use.
- Not a condition of forking.
- Not a condition of redistribution.
- Not a feature unlock.
- Not a royalty.
- Not tracked by the project.
- Not a way to gain priority support.
- Not a way to influence roadmap decisions.

---

## What We Do Not Do

- We do not track who has donated.
- We do not store donation records.
- We do not condition any feature on donation status.
- We do not grant additional rights to donors.
- We do not remove any obligation from donors.
- We do not share donation information with third parties.
- We do not display a supporter badge based on donation.
- We do not route telemetry or usage data to a donation processor.

## Privacy

The project does not process or store personal data in
connection with donations. If you choose to donate through a
third-party processor, that processor's privacy policy applies
to the transaction. The project does not receive personal data
unless you choose to share it.

The software itself has no knowledge of donation status. The
panel does not detect, query, or store whether you have
donated. The panel does not change any behavior based on
donation.

---

## For Forks

If you fork this project, you may:

- Keep the upstream donation channels as-is.
- Remove the upstream donation channels.
- Add your own donation channels.
- Combine upstream and own channels, provided the distinction
  is clear to users.

If you fork this project, you must:

- Preserve the upstream attribution.
- Not present upstream channels as your own.
- Not condition any feature on donation.
- Not track donation status in a way that affects feature
  availability or access.
